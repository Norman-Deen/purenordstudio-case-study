## Interactive Features

![Material configurator](images/purenordstudio-06.webp)
![Studio workflow](images/purenordstudio-08.webp)

Users can explore materials in real time, capture visuals, and create content directly inside the browser without switching tools.

The new White Clay mode presents the entire chair with a neutral finish, making its form easier to inspect. Width, height, and depth appear directly around the model, alongside interactive points that open design views, seat and frame connection details, and an assembly overview.

Dimensions and detail points remain visible while White Clay is selected, even when the material panel is closed. Switching to another finish returns to the material presentation.

Users can also enter and leave a staged room environment to explore the chair in context.

## Project Overview

PureNordStudio was built to move beyond traditional product presentation.

Instead of showing products as static images or simple 3D viewers, the system creates a complete interactive experience where users can explore, customize, and generate new visuals in real time.

The goal is to bring product presentation, interaction, and content creation into one unified workflow.

## Latest Improvements

- **Faster initial opening:** approximately 0.5–0.7 seconds in recent measurements by the author, with additional scene content loading progressively.
- **Clearer product inspection:** White Clay mode, visible model dimensions, and interactive design and assembly details.
- **Smoother navigation:** refined camera handling, room transitions, and controls during studio preview and recording.
- **More reliable capture:** improved recording cleanup and resizing behavior, with consistent Nourtin branding on images and video.
- **Better recovery:** clearer Retry and Reload actions when loading or browser graphics initialization fails.
- **More accessible interaction:** improved keyboard access, focus handling, and controls on narrow and landscape screens.
- **More reliable AI workflow:** improved cancellation, timeout handling, and feedback.
- **Maintenance and quality:** removed unused code and obsolete assets, expanded project documentation, and added automated checks before deployment.

These updates describe the latest project work; availability in the live demo depends on deployment.
